Let's set up your household

Holt runs entirely on this computer. Your statements, figures and notes never leave it, and the assistant is a local model with no network access.

You'll need A recent statement from each shared account: CSV, OFX or PDF. Three months is best. About 15 minutes. You can add anything you don't know yet later.
