# Suggestion

The one shape for anything the assistant or the app proposes: a job for a transaction, a cut, a plan change, review wording.

- **Who suggested it:** a small label, "Suggested by the assistant" or "Found by the app".
- **What:** one or two sentences in active voice. Every figure is calculated by the app, never written by the model.
- **Evidence:** a data-caption line naming how many transactions, which accounts and which period. Selecting it shows them.
- **Actions:** the accept action first, as a primary button with a specific verb ("I'll cancel it", "Add job"). Then the alternative (Change or Keep), then Dismiss as a quiet button.
- **Nothing changes until accepted.** After acceptance, Undo appears in place.
- **Tone:** no praise, no alarm, no exclamation marks.
