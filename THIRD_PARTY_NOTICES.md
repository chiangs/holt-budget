| Asset | Licence | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| [Figtree](https://github.com/erikdkennedy/figtree) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | Bundled from `@fontsource/figtree`, which ships the font files and `LICENSE` (OFL). |
